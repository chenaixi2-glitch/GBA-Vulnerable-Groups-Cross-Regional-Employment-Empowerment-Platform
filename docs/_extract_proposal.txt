(5)Businessmodel
Corepositioningandunderlyingprinciples
The first public welfare-themed + market-oriented employment empowerment
platform for disadvantaged groups across regions in the Greater Bay Area. In the
early stage, it will build an industry benchmark and user/enterprise trust through free
one-stop services. In the later stage, it will achieve profitability by providing value-
added services to B-end entities (enterprises, governments, institutions), all of which
will be used to reinvest in the upgrade of public welfare services and the expansion
of service scope. Eventually, it will become a "self-sustaining, continuously
empowering"socialvalue-orientedemploymentserviceplatform.
1. Zero-cost for C-end users: All basic services for disadvantaged groups (resume
optimization, job matching, free skills courses, policy consultation) and core value-
addedservices(one-on-oneguidance,precisejobrecommendations)arefreeforlife,
16
withnohiddencharges.
2. Public welfare first, with bounded profit: Profits are only for B-end entities, and the
service content is centered around "improving employment matching efficiency,
assisting corporate public welfare brands, and promoting policy implementation",
without providing any discriminatory or utilitarian services. The principle of "de-
labeling"and"equalemployment"isadheredto.
3. Profit funds are dedicated: Set up an independent public welfare fund account. All
profit income is only used for platform technology iteration, expansion of public
welfare services, exclusive benefits for disadvantaged groups, and establishment of
offline service points. The usage of funds is regularly disclosed and subject to
supervision by the University of Hong Kong, public welfare organizations, and
cooperatingenterprises.
4. Light asset operation: The entire process relies on the existing platform's digital
resources, the cooperation network in the Greater Bay Area, and the professional
capabilities of the team. No additional heavy asset investment is made. The profit
model is mainly "service output, resource connection, brand empowerment",
reducingoperationalrisks.
5. Gradual advancement, step-by-step: The exploration of profitability is deeply
bound to the operation rhythm of the platform. Payable services are launched in
stagesandsteps,inordertoensurethatthequalityandreputationoftheinitialpublic
welfareservicesarenotaffectedbytheexplorationofprofitability.
