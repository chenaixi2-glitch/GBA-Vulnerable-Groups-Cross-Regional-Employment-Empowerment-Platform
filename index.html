Frequently Asked Questions

The GBA Cross-Border Employment Empowerment Platform is an AI-powered recruitment platform designed to connect vulnerable groups with employment opportunities across the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. It uses advanced technology to eliminate employment discrimination, ensure fair matching, and provide comprehensive support throughout the employment process.

The "blind screening" feature automatically anonymizes candidates' personal information that could potentially lead to bias, such as age, gender, disability status, and photos. Employers see only skills, experience, and qualifications, helping to ensure fair and objective evaluation based purely on merit.

Yes, the platform is designed to be fully accessible. It complies with WCAG 2.2 standards and includes features such as screen reader compatibility, keyboard navigation, high contrast mode, text size adjustment, and a voice assistant for hands-free operation.

We strictly adhere to the principle of cross-border data localization and implement hierarchical privacy storage. The platform provides a blind screening mode to automatically hide users' sensitive information. All data transmission uses encryption technology, and privacy impact assessments are conducted regularly to ensure user data security.

For companies, this includes your company information and hiring needs. Once your profile is complete, you can start using our AI-powered features to find opportunities or candidates.
