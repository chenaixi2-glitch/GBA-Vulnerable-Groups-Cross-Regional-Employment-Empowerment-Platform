What visa do I need to work in Hong Kong if I'm from mainland China?

If you're from mainland China working in Hong Kong, you'll typically need an Employment Visa. The requirements include:

  • A confirmed job offer from a Hong Kong employer
  • Relevant qualifications and experience
  • The position should not be easily filled by local workers
  • A minimum salary level (currently around HK$15,000/month)

Your employer will usually sponsor your visa application. Processing time is typically 4-6 weeks.
