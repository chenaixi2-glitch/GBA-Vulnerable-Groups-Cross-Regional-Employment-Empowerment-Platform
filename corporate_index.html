Technical Questions
  1. Can you explain the difference between REST and GraphQL APIs?
  2. How would you optimize a database query that's performing poorly?
  3. Describe your experience with microservices architecture.
  4. How do you ensure code security in your development process?
  5. Explain a complex technical problem you solved and your approach.
Behavioral Questions
  1. Tell me about a time when you had to work with a difficult team member.
  2. Describe a situation where you had to meet a tight deadline.
  3. How do you handle feedback and criticism?
  4. Give an example of a project where you took initiative.
  5. How do you prioritize tasks when faced with multiple deadlines?
Case Study

Our company needs to improve the performance of our customer portal, which currently has slow page load times and poor mobile responsiveness. How would you approach this problem?

  • What information would you gather first?
  • How would you identify the root causes?
  • What solutions would you propose?
  • How would you prioritize implementation?
