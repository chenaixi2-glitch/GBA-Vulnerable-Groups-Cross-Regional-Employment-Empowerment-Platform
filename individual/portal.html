Frequently Asked Questions

We strictly adhere to the principle of cross-border data localization and implement hierarchical privacy storage. The platform provides a blind screening mode to automatically hide users' sensitive information such as age, disability status, and gender. All data transmission uses encryption technology, and privacy impact assessments are conducted regularly to ensure user data security.

Our AI matching algorithm analyzes your skills, experience, and preferences, and matches them with job requirements. It uses a "skills-first" approach that focuses on your qualifications rather than personal characteristics. The system can also operate in "blind screening" mode to remove potentially bias-inducing information from your profile.

Yes, the platform is designed to be fully accessible. It complies with WCAG 2.2 standards and includes features such as screen reader compatibility, keyboard navigation, high contrast mode, text size adjustment, and a voice assistant for hands-free operation.

Our AI Interview Coaching feature provides interview simulations with industry-specific questions. It offers real-time feedback on your answers, body language, and communication style. We also provide cross-cultural communication training to help you navigate cultural differences in interviews.

Basic platform features are free for individual users. We offer premium services such as advanced resume optimization, unlimited interview coaching sessions, and priority matching for a subscription fee. Financial assistance is available for users with limited income.
